About Vyāsa Pūjā

Honouring the spiritual master

Vyāsa Pūjā is the most auspicious occasion to honour and glorify the spiritual master, who represents the disciplic succession dating back to Lord Krishna Himself. On this sacred day, disciples from all corners of the world offer their heartfelt prayers, gratitude and devotion at the lotus feet of His Holiness Radhanath Swami Maharaj.

Each appearance day, those offerings are gathered here — a living record of Maharaj's mission to share the teachings of Srila Prabhupada and to inspire countless souls on the path of bhakti-yoga.
