A letter to Gurudev, from wherever you are.

Each year, disciples and well-wishers write to Maharaj for his appearance day. Your words are gathered here with thousands of devotees to make a garland to honour Gurudev.

  1. १

    Write from the heart

    Share your gratitude, realisations or a prayer — in any language, as short or as long as you wish. Your draft is kept safely on your device while you write.

  2. २

    Read with care

    A small team of sevaks reads every offering before it is published, so that this remains a prayerful space.

  3. ३

    Offer to Gurudev

    Once approved, your offering appears along with those of devotees from around the world — and a new flower is added to the garland above.

“I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of my spiritual master.”

Honouring the spiritual master

Vyāsa Pūjā is the most auspicious occasion to honour and glorify the spiritual master, who represents the disciplic succession dating back to Lord Krishna Himself. On this sacred day, disciples from all corners of the world offer their heartfelt prayers, gratitude and devotion to His Holiness Radhanath Swami Maharaj.

Each appearance day, those offerings are gathered here — a living record of Maharaj's mission to share the teachings of Srila Prabhupada and to inspire countless souls on the path of bhakti-yoga.
