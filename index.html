ॐ अज्ञानतिमिरान्धस्य ज्ञानाञ्जनशलाकया ।
चक्षुरुन्मीलितं येन तस्मै श्रीगुरवे नमः ॥

To the spiritual master, who opened my eyes — blinded by the darkness of ignorance — with the torch of knowledge, I offer my respectful obeisances.

Each flower is a recent offering — hover over one to see whose.

A letter to Gurudev, from wherever you are.

Each year, disciples and well-wishers write to Maharaj. Your words are gathered here with thousands of devotees to make a garland to honour Gurudev.

  1. १

    Write from the heart

    Share your gratitude, realisations or a prayer — in any language, as short or as long as you wish. Your draft is kept safely on your device while you write.

  2. २

    Read with care

    A small team of sevaks reads every offering before it is published, so that this remains a prayerful space.

  3. ३

    Offer to Gurudev

    Once approved, your offering appears along with those of devotees from around the world — and a new flower is added to the garland above.

    वन्दे गुरूणां चरणारविन्दे

    “I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of my spiritual master.”

    Honouring the spiritual master

    The spiritual master represents the disciplic succession dating back to Lord Krishna Himself, and gratitude to him is the heart of a disciple's life. Each year, disciples from all corners of the world offer their heartfelt prayers, gratitude and devotion to His Holiness Radhanath Swami Maharaj.

    Those offerings are gathered here — a living record of Maharaj's mission to share the teachings of Srila Prabhupada and to inspire countless souls on the path of bhakti-yoga.
